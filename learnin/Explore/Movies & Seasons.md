🎬 Movies

**The Social Network (2010)** → Facebook’s messy origin story. Not technical-heavy, but motivating if you’re into startups + product building.

**The Imitation Game (2014)** → Alan Turing cracking Enigma. Historical, math-heavy, and a reminder of how computing started.

**Pirates of Silicon Valley (1999)** → The Apple vs Microsoft origin rivalry. Feels dated but chef’s kiss for software history.

**Hackers (1995)** → Okay, this one is cheesy AF, but it inspired an entire generation of coders. More cyberpunk vibes than accuracy, though.

**Ex Machina (2015)** → AI + ethics. Philosophical but great for thinking beyond just “coding.”

**WarGames (1983)** → Old-school, but the classic “kid hacks into military computer” vibe. Surprisingly influential in real cybersecurity culture.

**Hidden Figures (2016)** → Not strictly about software, but about women in computing/math at NASA. Inspiring af.



---

📺 TV / Web Series

**Halt and Catch Fire (2014–2017)** → Lowkey the best tech series. Follows PC industry + software evolution. Shows the grind of innovation.

**Mr. Robot (2015–2019)** → The most accurate depiction of hacking/cybersecurity. Dark, but realistic.

**Silicon Valley (2014–2019)** → Comedy but painfully accurate startup struggles (funding, scaling, dev chaos).

**StartUp (2016–2018)** → About blockchain/crypto + tech startups. More drama, but motivating if you’re eyeing fintech.

**Devs (2020)** → Trippy take on quantum computing + deterministic software. Not light, but thought-provoking.

**Inside Bill’s Brain (2019, Netflix docu)** → Bill Gates documentary — shows his problem-solving approach more than coding.



---

👉 If you want real inspo to learn software engineering, I’d rank:

1. **Halt and Catch Fire (long-form tech journey)**


2. **Mr. Robot (cybersecurity reality check)**


3. **The Social Network (startup grind inspo)**
